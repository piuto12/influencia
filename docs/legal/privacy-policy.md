# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 November 2026

This policy explains how **Eldo (Influencia Team)** ("we", "us") collects and uses personal data when you register for or attend **Influencia – Edition 3** ("the event"), and use this website.

## 1. Who is responsible
**Eldo**, NCAS, [STREET, CITY, PIN], is the data fiduciary for the personal data described here.
**Grievance contact:** Basil, eldokj@nilgiricollege.ac.in, office phone +91 8589990060. We aim to respond within 7 working days.

## 2. What we collect
When you register we collect what you enter in the form:
- Full name, email address, mobile number and WhatsApp number
- Organization or institution, designation, department or industry, and city
- Your participant category and whether you attended a previous Influencia
- The time you accepted this policy

When you pay we also collect:
- The payment method, amount, your transaction reference (for example a UPI/UTR number)
- Any payment screenshot or receipt you upload (this can contain your name, account or phone details)

During the event we record whether you checked in and when. After the event we may issue a certificate that carries your name and a unique certificate ID.

We do **not** collect card numbers, CVV or net-banking passwords. If online payment is offered, it is handled by Razorpay on its own secure page.

## 3. Why we use it
- To process your registration, check your payment and confirm your seat
- To contact you about your registration, payment, the event and your certificate (email, and WhatsApp or phone where you give us the number)
- To manage entry and attendance on the day
- To issue and verify certificates
- To keep accounting and audit records and to prevent fraud and duplicate registrations
- To improve the event, using summary figures (for example how many people attended)

Our legal basis is **your consent**, given when you tick the box on the registration form, and, where applicable, legitimate uses permitted by law. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 7); this may mean we cannot keep your seat or issue a certificate.

## 4. Who sees your data
- **Our team:** only authorised administrators, each with a role limited to what they need (for example only finance administrators see payment evidence). Sign-in for administrators requires two-step verification, and administrator actions are logged.
- **Service providers that process data for us:**
  - **Supabase** (database and file storage) — our project runs in India (Mumbai region)
  - **Vercel** (website hosting) — serving from India (Mumbai) where available
  - **Resend** (sending emails) — email delivery may involve processing outside India
  - **Razorpay** (online payments), only if you choose to pay online
  - **WhatsApp**: we only provide click-to-chat links; messages you or we send are handled by WhatsApp under its own policy
- **Public information:** a certificate verification page shows the name, programme, date and validity of a certificate to anyone who has its certificate ID. Photographs and videos from the event may be published (see the Terms).
- We do **not** sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it
- Registration and payment records: [RETENTION PERIOD — e.g. 7 years for accounting records; confirm with your accountant]
- Payment screenshots and receipts: 30 days after the event, then deleted
- Check-in and certificate records: [same as registration records]
- Server logs and security records: up to [12] months

After these periods we delete or anonymise the data, unless the law requires us to keep it.

## 6. Where and how it is protected
Data is stored in a database with row-level access controls; payment evidence is kept in a private store and opened only through short-lived links; connections use HTTPS; administrator accounts use two-step verification and every sensitive action is recorded. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 7. Your rights
You can ask us to: **see** the personal data we hold about you, **correct** it, **erase** it, or **withdraw consent**, and you can **nominate someone** to exercise your rights if you cannot. Write to eldokj@nilgiricollege.ac.in. We may need to confirm your identity first. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## 8. Children
If you are **under 18**, you may attend only with a parent or legal guardian's consent, given on your behalf before you register. We do not knowingly collect a minor's data without that consent.

## 9. Cookies
Visitors to this site are not given advertising or analytics cookies. Essential technical cookies are used only for administrator sign-in. Videos from YouTube or Vimeo load **only after you click play**, and then those services' own policies apply.

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced on this site.
